    Chapter 04 / 04 · The truth

    No smoke,no lies.Just a prototype.

    Jorogumo is an educational prototype for the HyperBloom UI/UX hackathon. The three scenarios use curated examples of common tracker categories, not a live scan, browser inspection, or proof that a particular site uses them. For a real audit, try tools like Blacklight by The Markup or Privacy Badger. The visual style is original, inspired by Taishō-era Japan and cinematic anime.
